How a custom order works

Four steps, usually across one or two short conversations.

01

Tell us your idea

Fill out the form below with as much or as little detail as you have — even a vague idea is a great place to start.

02

Choose colors & details

We'll reply with a few questions and a rough quote, then confirm colors, size, and any reference photos.

03

We create your piece

Your piece is made by hand in our studio, usually within 7–12 business days depending on complexity.

04

You receive something made just for you

Packaged with care and shipped with tracking, ready to gift or keep.
