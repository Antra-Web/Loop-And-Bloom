Small stitches.
Big feelings.

Every piece that leaves our studio is made individually, by one pair of hands, on one hook, one loop at a time. Nothing is molded or mass-produced — which means every daisy curls a little differently, and every plushie's ear sits at its own particular angle.

That's not a flaw we're working out. It's the part we'd never automate away — the character of the person who made it, still there in the stitches when it reaches you.

From a strand of yarn to something you'll keep.

Four unhurried stages, repeated a little differently for every single order.

01

Choose the yarn

Every order starts with color — matched by hand against the mood, the occasion, or a photo someone sends us.

02

Shape every stitch

Rows build slowly into a shape — a petal, an ear, the curve of a bag's base — worked one loop at a time.

03

Add the little details

Faces are embroidered, petals are sewn on, ends are woven in — the slow work that makes a piece feel finished.

04

Finish with love

Every piece gets a final look-over, a gentle steam, and careful packaging before it heads to its new home.
